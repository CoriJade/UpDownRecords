ABOUT

Up/Down Records is an independent record label founded by the multi-talented Justin Furstenfeld, renowned as the lead singer and creative force behind the iconic band Blue October.

Established in 2010, with longtime managers Paul Nugent and Swin Swinford, Up/Down Records has been a labor of love, dedicated to nurturing and promoting all music from Blue October and Justin Furstenfeld.
